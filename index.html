01 About

About

Nan is a self-taught photographer with a passion for capturing moments wherever he goes. He shoots with whatever device is at hand, whether a smartphone or a camera. He thrives on variety, always chasing new places and fresh environments.

His experience ranges from graduation shoots to field trip documentation, with a special love for close-up portraits, landscapes, architectural shoots, and street photography.

In his spare time, Nan works as a freelance photographer and videographer. Beyond the lens, Nan likes to tinker with code. He's still learning, and he has fun exploring every new way to build things.
Fact this website was created by Nan himself, as part of his coding hobby.

Photography isn't just a job for me, it's my favorite thing to do.
